Developing the Visual Language for Meta AR Displays

September 9, 2026

Crafting Meta’s Design Language for AR Displays. Role: Design Strategy & Creative Direction.

The premise

AR designs were heavily influenced by VR — it didn’t work quite well. I led a team to explore an AR display native design language.

Material explorations

Not all renders are mine.

Aerogel became the metaphor

Given the versatility & it’s visual similarity with our displays, Aerogel became our metaphor; thus the Aero Design Language is born.

Motion explorations

Aero motion uses a soft ease-out that alleviates colour break-up artifacts on the display.

Aero panels are materially coupled — motion in one surface influences the rest, helping guide your attention.

Audio design

Aero audio uses higher-frequency sounds to cut through outdoor ambient noise and remain perceptible.

Shipped

Aero designs are shipped with Orion and subsequent wearable display products. The language evolved across generations as display fidelity improved.
