Crafting Meta's Design Language for AR

Project Lead  ·  Reality Labs  ·  2023–2024

Orion is Meta's first AR device. The team didn't have any precedence of a design language that works on transparent displays. I assembled a working team to establish the Design Language for AR.

Visual Language

Aero UI was inspired by aerogel—the world’s lightest, semi-opaque material...

Fast Company

The material explorations led to the discovery of Aerogel, a versatile material that helps embody our design intent. And thus, aerogel became the material metaphor and the Aero Design Language is born.

Motion Language

Aero motion uses a soft ease-out that alleviates colour break-up artifacts on the display.

Aero panels are materially coupled — motion in one surface influences the rest, helping guide your attention.

Motion doesn’t happen in isolation

Sound Language

Aero audio uses higher-frequency sounds to cut through outdoor ambient noise and remain perceptible.

Shipped

Aero designs are shipped with Orion and subsequent wearable display products. The language evolved across generations as display fidelity improved.
